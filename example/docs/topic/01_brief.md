## 1. What we answer

One sentence: given the constraints, which path do we choose, and what are the costs and risks.

- Constraints: the time window, available sources, and the irreversible-operations list (see `WORKSPACE.md §3`).
- Output: a conclusion report plus an evidence table.

## 2. What we do not answer

- We do not evaluate unrelated domains.
- We do not judge rumors that cannot be checked.

## 3. The deliverable

| Deliverable | Criterion | Who accepts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conclusion report | every conclusion has a citation or is marked `[inferred]` | project owner |
| Evidence table | one card per source, with a locatable excerpt | self-check plus cross-review |

## 4. Decomposition

One unit of work = one source → one evidence card. Finishing a card closes a session; do not chase several sources in one session.

- Inside a unit: read that source only, produce the card.
- Outside a unit: comparison and trade-offs belong to a summary session, so you do not conclude while still reading.
